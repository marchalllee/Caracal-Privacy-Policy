# Caracal-Privacy-Policy
Privacy Policy for Caracal

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Sahara Games ("we", "us", or "our") developed and published Caracal.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use the Caracal mobile game.

Information We Collect

Caracal uses third-party advertising services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). These services may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

- Approximate location
- Device or other identifiers, such as the advertising ID
- App interactions
- Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be collected or shared with third-party service providers for purposes including:

- Advertising and marketing
- Analytics
- Fraud prevention, security, and compliance

Caracal does not require you to create an account and does not provide user account registration or login.

Advertising

Caracal uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may use information collected from your device to provide, measure, and improve advertising, as well as for fraud prevention and security purposes.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's privacy information:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Information transmitted by the advertising services used by Caracal is encrypted in transit.

Data Deletion

Caracal does not provide user accounts or store personal user profiles on our own servers. Therefore, there is no user account or personal profile that can be deleted through Caracal.

Third-party services used by the game may have their own data retention and deletion policies.

Children's Privacy

Caracal is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
